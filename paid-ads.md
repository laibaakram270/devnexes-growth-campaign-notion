## 1. LinkedIn Ads Plan
**Campaign Objective**: Lead Generation - Free Trial Signups
**Audience**:
- Job Titles: Operations Manager, Co-Founder, Head of Operations, Content Lead
- Company Size: 10-50 employees
- Industries: Marketing Agency, SaaS, E-commerce
- Locations: Pakistan, UAE, Remote

**Ad Set 1: Agency Angle**
- **Ad Copy 1**: "Agencies: Replace 5 tools with 1. Cut SaaS costs by 60% with Notion for Teams. Get the free Agency Template Pack."
- **Ad Copy 2**: "Still using Drive + Slack + Asana? Centralize everything in Notion. Book a 15-min demo."
- **Visual**: Carousel showing "Before: 5 tabs open" vs "After: 1 Notion workspace"

**Ad Set 2: Startup Angle**
- **Ad Copy 1**: "Founders: Onboard new hires in 1 day with a Notion company wiki. Start 14-day free trial."
- **Ad Copy 2**: "Investor-ready in 30 days. Build your startup wiki + OKRs in Notion."
- **Visual**: Single image of clean Notion dashboard

**Budget**: $300 | **Bidding**: CPC | **KPI**: $8 CPL, 100 clicks
