# Week 1: Persona & Funnel Mapping - Notion for Teams

## 1. Product Chosen
**Notion for Teams** - All-in-one workspace for docs, wiki, tasks, and databases for 10-50 person teams.

## 2. Target Customer Personas / ICP

### Persona 1: "Agency Ops Manager Ayesha"
- **Job Title**: Operations Manager at 15-person Marketing Agency
- **Age**: 28-35 | **Location**: Lahore, Karachi, Dubai
- **Pain Points**: Tool overload, missed deadlines, client info scattered in Slack + Drive
- **Goals**: Cut SaaS costs, centralize SOPs, improve team visibility
- **Where they hang out**: LinkedIn, Agency Facebook Groups, Twitter/X
- **Decision Driver**: ROI, Cost savings, Team adoption

### Persona 2: "Startup Founder Bilal"
- **Job Title**: Co-Founder, 10-person SaaS Startup
- **Age**: 25-32 | **Location**: Pakistan, Remote
- **Pain Points**: No documentation, onboarding new hires takes too long, investor reporting is messy
- **Goals**: Scale team, build company wiki, track OKRs
- **Where they hang out**: LinkedIn, Twitter/X, ProductHunt
- **Decision Driver**: Speed, Templates, Investor-readiness

### Persona 3: "Content Lead Sara"
- **Job Title**: Head of Content, 25-person E-commerce Brand
- **Age**: 26-34 | **Location**: Lahore
- **Pain Points**: Content calendar chaos, writers missing briefs, asset tracking
- **Goals**: Streamline content pipeline, reduce review cycles
- **Where they hang out**: LinkedIn, Instagram, Blog
- **Decision Driver**: Ease of use, Templates, Collaboration
