#30-Day Growth Marketing Campaign
### Notion for Teams

**Tools:** Notion, LinkedIn Ads, Google Ads, Mailchimp, GA4, Excel

---

## Project Overview
This project is a complete end-to-end growth marketing campaign for "Notion for Teams". 
The goal was to acquire 15 new paying customers with a $500 budget and achieve 4.4x ROI.

## Deliverables
| Week | Deliverable | File |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ICP + Funnel Mapping | `persona-funnel.md` |
| 2 | 30-Day Content Calendar | `content-calendar.md` |
| 3 | Paid Ads + Email Drip | `paid-ads.md`, `email-drip.md` |
| 4 | CAC/LTV Model + Analytics | `analytics-report.md`, `week4-cac-ltv-model.xlsx` |

## Key Results
- **CAC**: $33.33
- **LTV**: $180 
- **ROI**: 4.4x
- **Payback Period**: 2.2 months
